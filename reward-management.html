QA CASE STUDY

Reward Management Platform

Led manual quality validation for customer-facing and internal applications supporting reward eligibility, fulfillment, customer-service workflows, and backend data processing.

Overview

Supported quality assurance for a reward-management platform that allowed customers to receive gift-card rewards after completing qualifying purchases or services. The solution included a customer-facing website, internal support applications, backend databases, and integrations used to process and display reward information.

Testing required validating the complete workflow across multiple systems to ensure eligibility, reward amounts, transaction status, and customer information remained accurate throughout processing.

Business Workflow

Customer completes a qualifying purchase or service
↓
Eligibility and reward rules are evaluated
↓
Reward request is processed through integrated systems
↓
Transaction and customer data are updated
↓
Reward information is displayed to customers and support teams

My Role

  • Reviewed requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and business workflows to identify testing scope and risk.
  • Created, maintained, and executed manual test cases in Azure DevOps.
  • Planned functional, regression, integration, exploratory, and end-to-end workflow coverage.
  • Validated customer-facing functionality and internal applications used by customer-support teams.
  • Used SQL queries to verify customer data, reward eligibility, transaction status, offer assignments, and reward amounts.
  • Documented defects with reproducible steps, supporting evidence, risk details, and business impact.
  • Retested resolved defects and assessed related regression risk before release.
  • Communicated test progress, quality concerns, and release-readiness recommendations to cross-functional stakeholders.

Testing Approach

Workflow Validation

Validated complete customer and support-user workflows across connected applications, including eligibility, reward requests, processing, fulfillment, and status updates.

Data Integrity

Used SQL queries to verify that customer, offer, reward, and transaction data remained accurate as information moved between application and database layers.

Risk-Based Regression

Prioritized high-risk workflows, integrations, and business rules when selecting regression coverage for enhancements, defect fixes, and release validation.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Reward information moved through multiple customer-facing, internal, and backend systems. A processing or synchronization failure could display an incorrect reward amount, status, or eligibility result to customers and support representatives.

Solution: Designed end-to-end scenarios that followed transactions across the full workflow. Combined UI validation with SQL verification, negative testing, targeted regression coverage, and defect analysis to identify where data or status changes stopped matching expected business behavior.

Tools & Test Coverage

Test Management

Azure DevOps for test-case management, requirement traceability, execution evidence, defect tracking, and fix verification.

Data Validation

SQL queries used to verify backend records, test data, transaction movement, reward amounts, offer assignments, and application-to-database consistency.

Application Validation

Browser-based functional, regression, integration, exploratory, negative, and cross-environment testing of customer-facing and internal applications.

Quality Impact

Increased confidence in reward processing by validating critical customer and support workflows across multiple application and database layers. Identified data, status, eligibility, and integration defects before release, reducing the risk of inaccurate reward information reaching customers or support teams.

Supported informed release decisions by communicating test coverage, unresolved risk, defect status, and verification results throughout delivery.
