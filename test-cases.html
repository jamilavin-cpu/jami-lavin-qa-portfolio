Test Design Philosophy

I design test cases that are clear, repeatable, and aligned with business requirements. My goal is to provide comprehensive coverage while making execution straightforward for QA teams, business users, and stakeholders. Each test case focuses on validating expected behavior, identifying risks, and supporting consistent, high-quality software releases.
