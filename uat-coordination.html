QA CASE STUDY

User Acceptance Testing Coordination

Coordinated business-user validation by preparing structured UAT materials, leading walkthroughs, supporting execution, managing reported issues, and helping teams reach informed release decisions.

Overview

Coordinated User Acceptance Testing activities for business-facing changes by translating requirements into clear validation steps, preparing users for execution, and providing support throughout testing.

The goal was to help business users confirm that delivered functionality supported real operational needs while ensuring questions, defects, and release risks were documented and addressed before sign-off.

UAT Process

Review user stories, requirements, and acceptance criteria
↓
Define business scenarios and expected outcomes
↓
Prepare UAT presentations and step-by-step materials
↓
Conduct walkthroughs with business users
↓
Support execution and clarify questions
↓
Triage reported issues and document confirmed defects
↓
Verify fixes, confirm results, and support sign-off

My Role

  • Reviewed user stories, business requirements, and acceptance criteria to determine UAT scope and expected outcomes.
  • Created PowerPoint presentations that documented the user story, business scenario, testing steps, and expected results.
  • Led walkthrough sessions with Customer Success and other business users before UAT execution began.
  • Explained workflow behavior, answered questions, and clarified expectations throughout testing.
  • Reviewed reported issues to distinguish defects from data, environment, requirement, or training-related questions.
  • Documented confirmed defects in Azure DevOps with reproducible details, supporting evidence, and business impact.
  • Coordinated with developers and stakeholders as issues moved through investigation and resolution.
  • Verified completed fixes and supported final UAT confirmation, release readiness, and sign-off.

Key Contributions

Business-Ready Test Guidance

Converted technical requirements into clear, step-by-step validation activities that business users could follow confidently.

UAT Facilitation

Prepared users for testing, led walkthroughs, remained available during execution, and helped maintain progress toward sign-off.

Defect Triage & Verification

Assessed reported issues, documented confirmed defects, coordinated resolution, and verified fixes against business expectations.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Business users needed to validate functionality while also managing their normal responsibilities. Unclear steps, unfamiliar workflows, or incomplete context could slow execution and make reported issues difficult to evaluate.

Solution: Prepared concise walkthrough materials, demonstrated the expected workflow before testing, stayed available for questions, and reviewed reported issues before escalation. This helped users focus on business validation while giving the delivery team clearer, more actionable feedback.

Tools & Deliverables

Planning & Tracking

Azure DevOps for user stories, acceptance criteria, defect documentation, status tracking, and verification results.

UAT Materials

PowerPoint walkthroughs, business scenarios, step-by-step instructions, expected outcomes, and sign-off support.

Collaboration

Customer Success, business users, product owners, developers, analysts, and QA team members.

Quality Impact

Improved UAT effectiveness by giving business users clear testing guidance, structured preparation, and responsive execution support. Helped identify defects and requirement gaps before release while reducing confusion around expected system behavior.

Supported stronger release decisions by providing documented UAT results, verified fixes, clear defect status, and confirmation that critical business workflows met user expectations.
