QA CASE STUDY

User Acceptance Testing Coordination

Supporting business users through structured UAT preparation, execution, defect validation, and communication throughout the software delivery process.

Overview

Coordinated User Acceptance Testing activities by preparing documentation, reviewing requirements, guiding business users through testing scenarios, and supporting defect resolution before release.

UAT Process

Review User Stories & Acceptance Criteria
↓
Create UAT Testing Materials
↓
Conduct User Walkthrough Sessions
↓
Support Business User Testing
↓
Validate Defects & Confirm Fixes

My Role

  • Created PowerPoint presentations outlining user stories and testing steps.
  • Conducted walkthrough sessions with customer success teams before testing began.
  • Provided support and answered questions throughout UAT execution.
  • Reviewed reported issues to determine whether they represented actual defects.
  • Entered and tracked defects through Azure DevOps.
  • Verified completed fixes and supported release readiness.

Key Contributions

Stakeholder Communication

Translated technical requirements into understandable testing activities for business users.

Test Planning

Created organized UAT materials to guide users through validation.

Defect Triage

Analyzed reported issues and partnered with development teams on resolution.

Tools Used

Planning & Tracking

Azure DevOps, User Stories, Test Documentation, and Defect Tracking.

Collaboration

Business stakeholders, product owners, developers, and QA teams.

Deliverables

UAT test plans, execution support, defect triage, and release readiness activities.

Quality Impact

Improved UAT effectiveness by providing clear testing guidance, supporting business users throughout validation, and ensuring issues were identified and addressed before release.
